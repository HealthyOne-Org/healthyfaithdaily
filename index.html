Questions

Good to know

What's the difference between Healthy Faith and Faith Daily Bible?

Faith Daily Bible is a free Bible reader: 17 translations, audio, the verse of the day and reading plans, with no ads and no account. Healthy Faith is the study app: word study you can ask questions of, sermon notes from a recording or a YouTube link, guided Scripture paths for anxiety, grief and doubt, a prayer journal, and circles for your church. Many people use both.

Is Healthy Faith free?

Reading the Bible, following plans and circles are free. Premium adds audio listening, the Ask feature and sermon notes for $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year, and starts with a 30-day free trial.

Which translations are included?

Healthy Faith reads the King James Version with Strong's numbers and the Berean Standard Bible. Faith Daily Bible includes NIV, NLT, ESV, NKJV, KJV, NASB, AMP, TPT, WEB and Spanish translations including RVR and NVI, licensed through YouVersion and API.Bible.

Does "Ask" replace my pastor?

No. Every answer is grounded in the Scripture and the Strong's entry you're looking at, and it says so when a question is better taken to a pastor. It's a study tool, not an authority.

Is it on Android?

Yes. Both apps are on Google Play. Healthy Faith is on the App Store today; Faith Daily Bible for iPhone is in review and will be listed here the day it's approved.

Who makes these apps?

Hardaway Labs LLC, a small studio that also makes HealthyOne. Chris Hardaway builds and runs them. You can reach us at support@healthyoneapp.com.
