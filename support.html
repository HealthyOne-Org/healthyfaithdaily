Your subscription

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by the store you bought from. iPhone or iPad: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Healthy Faith. Android: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before your renewal date to avoid the next charge.

I paid but the app still shows the paywall.

Open the app's settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. If that doesn't fix it, email us with the email on your account and the date of the charge.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are issued by Apple or Google. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Google Play order history. If you're unsure, email us and we'll help.

Your account and notes

Do my notes, highlights and sermons sync?

Yes, when you're signed in. Notes, highlights, bookmarks and sermons sync between the Healthy Faith app, the web app at healthyfaithdaily.com/app and Healthy Faith Study on your computer. Sign in with the same account everywhere.

I can't sign in.

Sign in the same way you did the first time: with Apple, with Google, or with your email. Using a different one creates a separate account, and your notes won't show. If you still can't get in, email us from the address on your account and we'll help.

How do I delete my account and data?

Use the account deletion page, or email us from the address on your account. See our privacy policy for what we keep and why.

Healthy Faith Study for Windows and Mac

What is Healthy Faith Study?

Healthy Faith Study is our desktop Bible study app for pastors and teachers. It has commentaries, cross-references, Hebrew and Greek word study, parallel Bibles and search, all free. AI study help and the sermon writer come with Healthy Faith Premium. It uses the same account as the Healthy Faith app.

How do I install it on Windows?

Get it from the Microsoft Store: go to healthyfaithdaily.com/download and click Get it for Windows, or open the Store, search for Healthy Faith Study, and click Get. It updates automatically. It works on Windows 10 and 11, on regular and ARM PCs.

How do I install it on a Mac?

Go to healthyfaithdaily.com/download and click Download for Mac. Open the file and drag Healthy Faith Study into your Applications folder. There's a version for M1 and newer Macs and one for older Intel Macs. It's signed and checked by Apple, so it opens without a warning.

Something isn't working on my computer.

Email us with what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps a lot. Mention whether you're on Windows or Mac.

Faith Daily Bible

Is Faith Daily Bible really free?

Yes. Reading, audio, the verse of the day and reading plans are free, with no ads and no account needed.

Audio won't play.

Check that your phone isn't on silent and that you're online. Audio streams for each chapter, so it needs an internet connection. If it still won't play, email us the translation and chapter.
