Installing

Windows

  1. Click Get it for Windows. The Microsoft Store opens.
  2. Click Get (or Install).
  3. Open Healthy Faith Study from the Start menu. It updates itself.

Mac

  1. Click Download for Mac and open the file.
  2. Drag Healthy Faith Study into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it from Applications. It's signed and checked by Apple.
